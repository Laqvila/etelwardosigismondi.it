    Post-earthquake reconstruction

    From the 2023 Reconstruction Decree (Sigismondi rapporteur) to the schools of the earthquake area and the framework law on post-disaster reconstruction

    Last update of the dossier: 12 March 2025 · Texts translated from Italian; acts and sources remain in the original language.

    Current status

    Concluded

    The framework law on post-disaster reconstruction (S.1294, Sigismondi rapporteur in Committee and in the Assembly) was given final approval by the Senate on 12 March 2025 with 83 votes in favour, 0 against and 29 abstentions: Law no. 40 of 18 March 2025. DL 3/2023 had already become Law no. 21 of 10 March 2023.

    12 March 2025

    Why it matters

    DL 3/2023, for which Sigismondi was rapporteur, unblocks stalled building sites and gives permanent posts to the staff of the reconstruction offices; schools in the 2016 earthquake area may form classes by derogation (528 thousand euros to Abruzzo in 2023/24); since 2025 a framework law sets uniform rules.

    Timeline

    1. 11 January 2023

      Bill S.462 converting DL no. 3 of 11 January 2023 (urgent measures on reconstruction following disasters and on civil protection), on the initiative of Meloni and Musumeci, tabled in the Senate; referred to the 8th Committee in reporting capacity on 12 January 2023. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    2. 17 January 2023

      The appointment of Etelwardo Sigismondi as rapporteur for bill S.462 in the 8th Committee is placed on record. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    3. 25 February 2023

      Examination of the amendments to the Reconstruction Decree concluded in the 8th Committee: extension to the areas hit by the 2016/2017 earthquakes of the simplifications provided for 2009, permanent posts for temporary staff, VAT advances to businesses, derogation from the minimum number of pupils per class. Press release

    4. 28 February 2023

      Assembly, sitting no. 43: Sigismondi appointed majority rapporteur (Committee afternoon sitting no. 25), oral report authorised, general debate. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    5. 1 March 2023

      Assembly, sitting no. 44: the Senate approves bill S.462 (amended from the proposer’s text) by a show of hands and transmits it to the Chamber of Deputies. The press release on the site (dated 2 March) reports Sigismondi’s statement on the floor as rapporteur. Press release

    6. 7 March 2023

      The Chamber of Deputies (C.930) gives final approval: Law no. 21 of 10 March 2023 (Official Gazette no. 60 of 10 March 2023). Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    7. 10 March 2023

      Press conference in L’Aquila (Emiciclo) with Sen. Liris and Commissioner Castelli to present the Reconstruction Decree. Press release

    8. 21 March 2023

      Statement on the sidelines of the press conference by Minister Musumeci and Commissioner Castelli: simplification to open building sites in the Central Italy earthquake area. Press release

    9. 30 March 2023

      The press release reports the go-ahead from the Finance Committee of the Chamber of Deputies (29 March) for the amendment to the decree on Superbonus tax credits, which exempts the areas hit by the Central Italy earthquakes and the 2022 flood from the block on credit transfers. Press release

    10. 18 April 2023

      Sigismondi and Liris meet Minister Musumeci and the director of Casa Italia, Ferrara, to bring the Abruzzo experience to bear on the drafting of the framework law on reconstruction. Press release

    11. 31 August 2023

      As a result of the amendment to the Reconstruction Decree tabled by Sigismondi as rapporteur (classes by derogation in the schools of the 2016 earthquake area until the 2028/2029 school year), the Ministry of Education allocates 528,184.41 euros to Abruzzo for the 2023/2024 school year. Press release

    12. 8 November 2024

      Bill S.1294 “Framework law on post-disaster reconstruction” (on the initiative of Minister Musumeci), approved by the Chamber of Deputies on 6 November 2024 (C.1632), transmitted to the Senate; referred to the 8th Committee in reporting capacity on 19 November 2024. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    13. 11 December 2024

      The appointment of Sigismondi as rapporteur for bill S.1294 in the 8th Committee is placed on record; on 4 March 2025 appointed majority rapporteur for the Assembly. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    14. 12 March 2025

      Assembly, sitting no. 284: oral report by the rapporteur, examination of Articles 1 to 28, final vote: given final approval (83 in favour, 0 against, 29 abstentions). Law no. 40 of 18 March 2025 (Official Gazette no. 76 of 1 April 2025). Sources (external link, opens in a new window)
