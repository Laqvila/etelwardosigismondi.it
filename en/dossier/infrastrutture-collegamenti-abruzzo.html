    Infrastructure and transport links

    The Pescara–Rome railway, Strada dei Parchi (A24–A25), the safety of the Trignina and the bridge over the Trigno

    Last update of the dossier: 29 April 2026 · Texts translated from Italian; acts and sources remain in the original language.

    Current status

    Update

    Latest step: the amendment to the Commissioners Decree approved by the 8th Committee on 29 April 2026, allocating €20.5 million for the bridge over the Trigno and €7 million for the Sente-Longo viaduct. For the Pescara–Rome railway the timeline is based on press releases; no subsequent parliamentary act has been verified.

    29 April 2026

    Why it matters

    Links that decide whether Abruzzo stays connected: the Pescara–Rome railway with the first tenders launched in 2023, the settlement of the A24–A25 dispute in the Anticipi Decree (Law 191/2023) and the Ministry of Infrastructure’s commitment on the safety of the SS 650 Trignina, secured through a parliamentary question.

    Timeline

    1. 30 January 2023

      Meeting in Pescara, with President Marsilio, with the Commander General of the Port Authorities Corps, Nicola Carlone: the Abruzzo port system and the upgrading of the Adriatic corridor. Press release

    2. 15 February 2023

      Oral question 3-00216 (Sigismondi, Della Porta) to the Minister of Infrastructure on the dualling and safety of the SS 650 Fondo Valle Trigno “Trignina” published (it replaces question 3-00197 of 2 February, withdrawn the same day). Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    3. 4 May 2023

      Question 3-00216 is taken in the Assembly (sitting no. 65): Under-Secretary Ferrante gives assurances that the inclusion of the works in the multi-year MIT–Anas plan will be assessed, along with safety measures. Press release

    4. 28 June 2023

      Sigismondi, Liris and Testa: tenders launched for the works to double the Pescara–Rome railway line. Press release

    5. 24 July 2023

      In Avezzano, a review of the Pescara–Rome lots with President Marsilio, RFI chief executive Strisciuglio, Commissioner Macello and Deputy Minister Bignami. Press release

    6. 28 July 2023

      Sigismondi: the funding for speeding up the Pescara–Rome line is being rescheduled with funds other than the PNRR; the project goes ahead. Press release

    7. 18 October 2023

      Bill S.912 converting DL no. 145 of 18 October 2023 (Anticipi Decree) tabled in the Senate. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    8. 1 December 2023

      Sigismondi: the amendment to the Anticipi Decree allowing Strada dei Parchi (Toto group) to return as concessionaire of the A24–A25, with 500 million recognised over 2023–2024 and tolls frozen at 2017 levels, approved in the Senate Budget Committee. Press release

    9. 7 December 2023

      The Senate approves bill S.912 (amended from the proposer’s text) and transmits it to the Chamber of Deputies. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    10. 14 December 2023

      The Chamber of Deputies (C.1601) gives final approval: Law no. 191 of 15 December 2023 (Official Gazette no. 293 of 16 December 2023). Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    11. 2 April 2026

      After the collapse of the bridge over the river Trigno on the SS 16, the motorway toll between the Vasto Sud and Termoli exits was suspended for the duration of the emergency, obtained together with Senator Costanzo Della Porta: the A14 becomes the alternative corridor for diverted traffic. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    12. 29 April 2026

      The Senate's 8th Committee approves the amendment to the Commissioners Decree: €20.5 million over two years for the design and reconstruction of the bridge over the Trigno, and €7 million for 2027 to secure and reopen the Sente-Longo viaduct, closed for eight years. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)
