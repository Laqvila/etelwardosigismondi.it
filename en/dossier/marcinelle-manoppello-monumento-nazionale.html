    Marcinelle: national monument at Manoppello

    Bill S.1877 to declare the Piazza Caduti di Marcinelle and the Chapel of the Victims a national monument, approved by the Senate and transmitted to the Chamber of Deputies

    Last update of the dossier: 3 August 2026 · Texts translated from Italian; acts and sources remain in the original language.

    Current status

    Ongoing

    Approved by the Senate on 3 August 2026 (sitting no. 445) unanimously by a show of hands, in a text amended from the proposer’s, and transmitted to the Chamber of Deputies as C.3069, which as at 5 September 2026 is recorded as “to be referred” to committee. It is not law.

    3 August 2026

    Why it matters

    National monument status for the square and the chapel at Manoppello protects the places that commemorate the Abruzzo victims of the Marcinelle mining disaster. The Senate voted for it unanimously; the procedure continues in the Chamber of Deputies.

    Timeline
