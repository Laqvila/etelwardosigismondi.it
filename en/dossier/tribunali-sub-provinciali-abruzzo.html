    The sub-provincial courts of Abruzzo

    Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona and Vasto: from annual extensions to the Government bill to make them permanent

    Last update of the dossier: 30 July 2026 · Texts translated from Italian; acts and sources remain in the original language.

    Current status

    Under examination

    The Government bill C.2646 “Provisions on judicial districts” (tabled in the Chamber of Deputies on 3 October 2025, referred to the II Justice Committee in reporting capacity on 29 October 2025) is under examination in Committee: on 30 July 2026 the base text was adopted, with examination continued and adjourned. Meanwhile the operation of the four courts for 2026 is guaranteed by DL 117/2025, converted by Law 148/2025 (final approval in the Senate on 1 October 2025).

    30 July 2026

    Why it matters

    Four courts (Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona, Vasto) remain open only thanks to annual extensions, the latest valid for 2026. The Government bill under examination in the Chamber of Deputies aims to make them permanent; since 2023 the possibility of supplementing their administrative staff has been law.

    Timeline

    1. 14 October 2022

      Bill S.188 “Amendments to Legislative Decree 155/2012 (new organisation of the ordinary courts)”, on the initiative of the Regional Council of Abruzzo, tabled in the Senate; referred to the 2nd Justice Committee on 26 January 2023, rapporteur Sen. Ernesto Rapani (appointed 12 April 2023). Last recorded step: 3 August 2023, under examination in committee. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    2. 15 February 2023

      The Senate approves bill S.452 converting DL 198/2022 (Milleproroghe), transmitted to the Chamber of Deputies; given final approval on 23 February 2023 (Law no. 14 of 24 February 2023). According to the press release of 21 June 2023, the Milleproroghe secured the extension of the courts’ operation until 1 January 2025. Press release

    3. 17 February 2023

      Visit with Sen. Liris to the Juvenile Court of L’Aquila and the former juvenile detention centre: shortages of staff and space; a dossier for the Minister announced. Press release

    4. 20 June 2023

      The Senate gives final approval to bill S.747 converting DL 44/2023 on strengthening the administrative capacity of public authorities (Law no. 74 of 21 June 2023). The press release of 21 June claims credit for the provision allowing the administrative staff of the courts of Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona and Vasto to be supplemented. Press release

    5. 22 November 2023

      Sigismondi speaks in the 2nd Justice Committee (morning sitting no. 99, on the Committee’s business, bill S.188 and related bills): Under-Secretary Ostellari announces a further extension for the four courts and a Government enabling law to reform the judicial map. Press release

    6. 30 November 2023

      Meeting in the Senate, organised by Sigismondi, Liris and Testa, between the rapporteur Rapani, the mayors, the presidents of the Provinces of Chieti and L’Aquila and the presidents of the Bar Councils of the four courts. Press release

    7. 28 December 2023

      The Council of Ministers approves the Milleproroghe decree (DL no. 215 of 30 December 2023) which, according to the statement by Sigismondi, Liris and Testa reported in the press, already contains in its text the extension of the courts of Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona and Vasto to 1 January 2026. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    8. 21 February 2024

      The Senate gives final approval to bill S.1027 converting DL 215/2023 (Milleproroghe 2024), already approved by the Chamber of Deputies on 19 February 2024 (Law no. 18 of 23 February 2024). Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    9. 10 January 2025

      Sigismondi and Liris: the Milleproroghe does not contain the extension because the aim is a definitive solution; Under-Secretary Delmastro, replying on the Senate floor on 9 January 2025, announced a draft Government bill to revise the judicial map. Press release

    10. 7 May 2025

      Meeting with Minister Nordio (attended by President Marsilio, mayors and Bar Councils): an extension beyond 2026 and a Government bill to make the four courts permanent announced. The press release of 22 July 2025 refers to this meeting as having taken place “in May”. Press release

    11. 22 July 2025

      The Council of Ministers approves the bill reorganising the judicial map, which provides for the definitive stabilisation of the courts of Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona and Vasto (joint statement by Sigismondi, Liris and Testa). Press release

    12. 1 October 2025

      The Senate gives final approval to bill S.1660 converting DL no. 117 of 8 August 2025 (urgent measures on justice), approved by the Chamber of Deputies on 24 September 2025 (Law no. 148 of 3 October 2025). The preamble of the decree lists among its purposes “ensuring the operation of the courts of Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona and Vasto for 2026 as well”. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    13. 3 October 2025

      Government bill C.2646 “Provisions on judicial districts” tabled in the Chamber of Deputies (Minister Nordio, jointly with Zangrillo and Giorgetti); referred to the II Justice Committee in reporting capacity on 29 October 2025. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)

    14. 30 July 2026

      II Justice Committee of the Chamber of Deputies: examination of bill C.2646 continued, with adoption of the base text and adjournment. Sources (external link, opens in a new window)
