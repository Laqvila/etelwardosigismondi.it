    Accessibility

    The site is designed to be usable with a keyboard, screen readers, magnification and mobile devices. Target: WCAG 2.2 level AA.

    Compliance status

    The site aims for compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA. At present compliance is partial: the criteria have been verified with automated and manual checks on the code and in a browser, not through a certified external audit.

    What has been verified

    Full keyboard navigation with an always-visible focus; skip link to the content; a single main heading per page and a regular heading hierarchy; text and control colour contrast of at least 4.5:1; alternative text for meaningful images; report form with explicit labels, declared required fields, errors linked to the fields and announced to screen readers; no content that moves by itself; respect for the reduced-motion preference; minimum control size of 24×24 pixels; content readable without JavaScript.

    Known limitations

    Press headlines in the review are given in their original language and may contain unexplained acronyms. Press-release images come from the previous archive and have alternative text limited to the release's title. The form's anti-spam check (Cloudflare Turnstile) is a third-party component: it almost always completes on its own, with no interaction; if it requires an action, its accessibility depends on Cloudflare, and anyone unable to send the form can write directly to the institutional email. External links lead to third-party sites whose accessibility does not depend on this site.

    Reporting

    If you encounter a barrier, write to the institutional address indicating the page and the problem: etelwardo.sigismondi@senato.it.
