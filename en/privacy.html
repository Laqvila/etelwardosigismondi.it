    Privacy

    The site does not track browsing and uses no profiling cookies. The only personal data processed is what you choose to send through the report form.

    Data controller

    The data controller is Senator Etelwardo Sigismondi, Senate of the Republic, Piazza Madama 1, 00186 Rome, Italy. For any matter concerning personal data: etelwardo.sigismondi@senato.it. No data protection officer has been appointed, as the conditions of Article 37 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 are not met.

    Browsing: no data collected by the site

    The site consists of static pages: it does not record browsing, uses no analytics or profiling tools, embeds no social plugins and hosts its own fonts. The infrastructure providers (see Recipients and providers) process technical connection data — IP address, browser type, page requested, date and time — to deliver the service and protect it from attacks, for the time strictly necessary and under their own notices. Legal basis: legitimate interest in the security and operation of the site (Article 6(1)(f)).

    Report form: which data and why

    By filling in the form on the Contacts page you send: name and surname, email address, subject and message (required); telephone number and municipality (optional, only if you provide them). Purpose: to follow up the report or message and reply to you. Legal basis: the processing is necessary to act on your request (Article 6(1)(b)) and meets the Senator's legitimate interest in receiving citizens' requests within the parliamentary mandate (point (f)). Providing the data is voluntary, but without the required fields no reply is possible. If your message contains special categories of data (for example about health), they are processed on the basis of the explicit consent given by sending it (Article 9(2)(a)): please write only what is necessary.

    How the message travels

    For now the form sends nothing from this site: the button opens your email program with the message already written, addressed to etelwardo.sigismondi@senato.it. The data travels from your email program to the Senator's institutional mailbox at the Senate of the Republic, without passing through this site's servers or any anti-spam service; the site's infrastructure neither receives, processes nor retains it.

    Recipients and providers

    The data is known to the Senator and to the staff of his office who handle correspondence. Infrastructure providers, processing only technical browsing data on behalf of the controller under Article 28: Cloudflare, Inc. (content delivery network, DNS, protection) and GitHub, Inc. (hosting of the static pages). The institutional mailbox is run by the systems of the Senate of the Republic under the Administration's rules. The data is neither disclosed nor transferred to third parties for commercial purposes.

    Transfers outside the European Union

    Cloudflare and GitHub are based in the United States and participate in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, recognised as adequate by the European Commission (decision of 10 July 2023); their data-processing agreements also include the standard contractual clauses. Pages are served from European nodes whenever possible.

    Retention

    The site retains no data. Emails received through the form are kept in the institutional mailbox for as long as needed to follow up the request and, in any case, no longer than 24 months from the last contact, unless their retention is required by law or to protect a right.

    Your rights

    You may at any time request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and object to it, as well as portability where applicable (Articles 15–22 of the Regulation), by writing to etelwardo.sigismondi@senato.it. If you believe the processing infringes the law, you may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (garanteprivacy.it) or apply to the courts.

    Minors and automated decisions

    The form is intended for people aged 14 or over. No profiling and no automated decision-making take place: every message is read and handled by people.

    Updates

    Notice updated on 10 September 2026. Changes will be published on this page with the new date.
