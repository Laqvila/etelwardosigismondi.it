    About

    About me

    An architect by training, from Vasto in Abruzzo, in the service of public institutions for nearly thirty years. Senator of the Republic since 2022.

    The mandate

    From the territory to Palazzo Madama

    Born in Vasto on 29 September 1974, he graduated in Architecture from the "G. D'Annunzio" University of Chieti-Pescara. His political commitment began at a very young age in the Fronte della Gioventù and continued in Azione Giovani, the youth organisation of Alleanza Nazionale, where he held provincial, regional and national roles: in 2004 he joined the National Executive chaired by Giorgia Meloni.

    He rose through eighteen years of local government — municipal councillor in Vasto, provincial councillor in Chieti — up to the post of private secretary to the President of the Abruzzo Region, Marco Marsilio. In 2012 he was among the founders of Fratelli d'Italia in Abruzzo, serving as its regional coordinator since 2015. Since 13 October 2022 he has been a Senator of the Republic for the Abruzzo constituency.

    Institutional career

    Since 1994, one direction

    1. From 1994

      Youth activism

      Fronte della Gioventù, then Azione Giovani: provincial, regional and national roles, up to the National Executive chaired by Giorgia Meloni in 2004.

    2. 1998 – 2016

      Vasto city councillor

      Elected for four terms in his home city, serving as delegate for youth policy, chair of the General and Institutional Affairs Committee and leader of his council group.

    3. 2009 – 2014

      Chieti provincial councillor

      Elected in the Vasto 1 constituency as the city's most voted candidate; he served as leader of the Fratelli d'Italia group.

    4. 2012

      Founder of Fratelli d'Italia in Abruzzo

      Among the founders of the party at regional level; he has been its regional coordinator since 2015.

    5. 2019 – 2022

      Private Secretary to the President of the Region

      At the side of President Marco Marsilio at the helm of the Abruzzo Region.

    6. Since 2022

      Senator of the Republic

      Elected for the Abruzzo constituency; since 9 November 2022 FdI Group Leader on the 8th Standing Committee. Since 2023 a member of the Committee for Regional Affairs and of the Anti-Mafia Committee.
