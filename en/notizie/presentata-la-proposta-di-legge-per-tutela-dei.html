    Trabocchi: a bill for their protection and an exemption from Bolkestein

    30 October 20232 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    Presented in Pescara: the bill protecting the trabocchi and traditional fishing techniques, with an exemption from the Bolkestein directive for concessions. Sigismondi is first signatory.

    Why it matters: The Regions could step in to repair trabocchi damaged by storms and high tides.

    A bill to protect the trabocchi, the so-called ‘traditional fishing machines’, converted into restaurant businesses and symbolic places for the Abruzzo coast, and beyond. It was presented today, during a press conference at the Senate, by the Abruzzo members of Parliament of Fratelli d’Italia, Etel Sigismondi, FdI regional coordinator, Guido Liris, and Guerino Testa. “The intent – explained Sigismondi – is to give the Regions the possibility to act on the consolidation, the enhancement and, if necessary, the reconstruction of the trabocchi themselves, given that episodes of damage to them due to bad weather or high tide are very frequent.

    But not only protection and maintenance – Sigismondi continues – because the bill also contains the request for a derogation from the Bolkestein rules, since these places cannot be treated in the same way as bathing establishments.” A derogation that is also an identity message that Fratelli d’Italia wants to send, as Senator Liris explains, given that “FdI, more than others, does not renounce the preservation and protection of the symbols that are integral parts of the history of the territories.

    Indeed, having the privilege of being members of Parliament makes it possible to achieve this objective concretely, providing tools that give rise to ‘best practices’ for the protection of a reality that has an exceptional history, with characteristics unique of their kind, and that we intend to preserve and hand down to the generations to come.” An identity that for Deputy Mariangela Matera also means protecting our Made in Italy: “The trabocchi represent the identity not only of Abruzzo but also of other Regions and must therefore absolutely be safeguarded, also from the standpoint of protecting our Made in Italy and tourism, which is fundamental for the economic growth of our Country.”

    But Fratelli d’Italia is already looking beyond the bill to offer the trabocchi broader protection, as Deputy Guerino Testa explains, for whom “the commitment to Abruzzo passes through this bill but will continue with the candidacy of the trabocchi as a Unesco heritage site. Another fundamental step to continue that work of enhancement and rebirth which, after years of neglect, Abruzzo is experiencing thanks to the work of President Marco Marsilio.”

    And finally there are those who look beyond Abruzzo, such as Senator Fallucchi, for whom “the trabocco is a cultural and tourist symbol of the Gargano as well. For this reason I joined this bill by Senator Sigismondi with conviction, and I will follow its process closely, providing the utmost contribution so that it becomes law as soon as possible,” she concludes. Present this morning at the press conference, in addition to the regional councillor for the sector, Nicola Campitelli, were also numerous Abruzzo mayors of the Costa dei Trabocchi.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.
