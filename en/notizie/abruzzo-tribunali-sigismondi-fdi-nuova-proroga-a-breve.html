    Abruzzo courts: new extension ahead of the judicial geography reform

    22 November 20231 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    New extension for the courts of Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona and Vasto, announced in the Senate Justice Committee on 22 November 2023; meeting with mayors and lawyers on 30 November.

    Why it matters: The four courts remain operational pending the reform of judicial geography.

    Part of the dossier: The sub-provincial courts of Abruzzo

    “The courts of Avezzano, Lanciano, Sulmona and Vasto will be extended once again; this is what emerged today in the Senate Justice Committee, in which I took part.” This was announced by the Abruzzo senator of Fratelli d’Italia, Etelwardo Sigismondi. “Undersecretary Andrea Ostellari, present in committee – he continues – also informed us of the presentation by the government of an enabling law for the reform of the judicial geography, which is about to come to the attention of Parliament.

    The further extension of the Abruzzo courts, therefore, is necessary in order to allow the review of the national judicial geography. I personally asked the Undersecretary – Sigismondi reports – that the extension procedure be included directly in the text drawn up by the government, without having to resort to amendments. I would remind you that on the agenda of the Justice Committee there is also the regional-initiative law for the rescue of our sub-provincial courts, on which, together with my colleague Guido Liris, and also at the request of President Marco Marsilio, we considered it important to hold a moment of in-depth discussion to provide information on the state of play.

    On this matter, in fact – he announces – we have organized a meeting with the rapporteur of the law, Sen. Ernesto Rapani, addressed to the mayors, the presidents of the Provinces of Chieti and L’Aquila and the presidents of the Bar Councils of the four Courts, which will take place at the Senate on the afternoon of next Thursday, 30 November,” Sigismondi concludes.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.

    Original title of the press release: Abruzzo – courts, Sigismondi (FdI): New extension. Enabling law for the reform of the judicial geography coming soon. Meeting with mayors, provinces and lawyers on the 30th
