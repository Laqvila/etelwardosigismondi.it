    Reconstruction decree: simplification to open worksites in the earthquake zone

    21 March 20231 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    The Reconstruction decree, presented at a press conference by Musumeci and Castelli on 20 March 2023, relies on simplification and firm timelines to open worksites in the Central Italy earthquake zone.

    Why it matters: Fewer bureaucratic steps to speed up reconstruction in the earthquake-hit areas.

    Part of the dossier: Post-earthquake reconstruction

    “The Reconstruction decree is a concrete response to the real needs of the territories hit by the earthquake. The citizens and public administrations of those territories can today count on a legislative text, wanted by the Meloni government, which sets a very clear objective: to speed up the reconstruction processes in order to recover lost time, moving from a planning phase to the opening of construction sites. One of the main regulatory strategies put in place is called ‘simplification’, to overcome bureaucratic obstacles and to ensure faster procedures and certainty over the timeframes of the works for recovery.

    A comprehensive and far-sighted measure that testifies to this government's determination to bring a tangible change of pace to the process of reconstruction and revival of the seismic crater of Central Italy”.

    This is what the Abruzzo senator of Fratelli d'Italia and group leader in the 7th Committee in the Senate, Etelwardo Sigismondi, declared yesterday morning on the sidelines of the press conference held by Minister Musumeci and the Extraordinary Commissioner for Reconstruction, Castelli.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.

    Original title of the press release: Territories at the center for a concrete change of pace with the Meloni Government
