    Reconstruction framework law: Abruzzo’s experience brought to Musumeci

    18 April 20231 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    Meeting on 18 April 2023 with Minister Musumeci: the Abruzzo Region’s experience offered to the reconstruction framework law, for uniform rules on emergency and recovery.

    Why it matters: Uniform rules for disaster-hit areas, including measures for social and economic recovery.

    Part of the dossier: Post-earthquake reconstruction

    “An important meeting, this morning, with the Minister for Civil Protection, Nello Musumeci, to whose attention we submitted the experience gained in the Abruzzo Region, in order to contribute to the drafting of the framework law on Reconstruction, also in the presence of the director of the Casa Italia department, Luigi Ferrara. We greatly appreciated the work that the Minister, with great determination, is carrying forward with the aim of guaranteeing uniform rules across the entire national territory regarding the management of the emergency and reconstruction phases in all areas hit by natural disasters.

    We considered it useful to offer our cooperation, together with the director of the Special Office for Reconstruction, Vincenzo Rivera, and we thank the Minister for the attention shown to our proposals. They are all aimed at creating a governance model that not only regulates repair and reconstruction works in the territories affected by seismic events but also lays out the actions connected to socio-economic revival. Our thanks also go to Mr Rivera, who placed his technical and specialist expertise at the service of our legislative proposal.”

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.

    Original title of the press release: Reconstruction framework law, Sigismondi and Liris (FdI) meet Minister Musumeci
