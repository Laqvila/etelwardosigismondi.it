    Bad weather in the Chieti area, Sigismondi replies to Menna and Legnini on the funds already allocated

    10 November 20263 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    On 10 November 2026 Sigismondi replies to the President of the Province of Chieti, Francesco Menna, and the Mayor of Chieti, Giovanni Legnini, on the damage from the bad weather of late March and early April. He recalls the 15 million allocated with the state of emergency declared on 9 April and, through the Commissioners Decree, the 20.5 million for the new bridge over the Trigno and the 7 million for the Sente-Longo viaduct. According to the Senator, the 150 million estimate cited by Menna concerns the reconstruction phase that follows the emergency.

    Why it matters: The spring bad weather brought down the bridge over the Trigno on state road 16 and closed roads across the province. The statement lists the funds already allocated and the steps the law sets out from emergency to reconstruction.

    Part of the dossier: Infrastructure and transport links

    “I am not surprised by the attitude of the President of the Province of Chieti and Mayor of Vasto, Francesco Menna, who has already shown on several occasions his administrative shortcomings and a questionable knowledge of the rules. What surprises me, instead, are the statements of the Mayor of Chieti, Giovanni Legnini, who as former extraordinary commissioner for reconstruction knows well the procedures laid down by civil protection legislation. For that very reason, rather than venturing into political exploitation, he would do better to explain them to his own party colleagues.

    After the exceptional wave of bad weather that hit Abruzzo, and the province of Chieti in particular, between the end of March and the beginning of April, the Abruzzo Region, led by President Marco Marsilio, immediately requested the recognition of a national state of emergency, which the Council of Ministers then declared on 9 April. As provided for by Article 24 of Legislative Decree no. 1 of 2018, the Civil Protection Code, the Government immediately allocated 15 million euro for assistance to the population, making the territories safe and the most urgent works.

    These resources were joined, through the Commissioners Decree, by 20.5 million euro for the design and reconstruction of the bridge over the Trigno river on state road 16, and 7 million euro for completing the safety works and reopening the Sente-Longo viaduct, a strategic infrastructure for the Alto Vastese and Molise. These are first measures, which may be followed by further allocations from the Council of Ministers once the damage survey is completed.

    How quickly the bodies concerned quantify their financial needs is of fundamental importance. If, as has happened, some emergency works are reported only months after the disaster, it is inevitable that the following procedures take longer. This is not about shifting responsibility onto mayors but about respecting the procedures laid down by law.

    It would rather be appropriate to understand why, on some roads for which the Province of Chieti is responsible, action was not taken promptly enough to restore connections, even though the rules allow emergency procedures to be used when the conditions are met. Menna should explain this, instead of attacking the other institutions.

    I would also recall that the national state of emergency lasts an initial maximum of twelve months, which can be extended for a further twelve, during which the most urgent needs are addressed. Once the emergency phase is over, reconstruction work begins. The 150 million euro cited by Menna is an estimate of overall needs, which cannot be confused with the resources for the first emergency phase; it concerns reconstruction work. Confusing and overlapping the emergency phase with the reconstruction phase is a great exercise in mystification by the centre-left towards mayors and citizens.

    President Marsilio was right to recall what happened in the past with other major emergencies, such as Rigopiano. But it would be enough to look at the hydrogeological instability in the Santa Maria district of Chieti too, where emergency works were followed by further reconstruction measures.

    It is therefore clear that, in the middle of an election campaign, Menna and his party colleagues are trying to attack the regional and national government. A telling example is the event organised last Saturday by Menna himself, which took on a clearly electoral character ahead of the imminent by-elections for the Chamber of Deputies, instead of being a genuine moment of institutional discussion. We are all committed to giving concrete answers to mayors and citizens, and for this very reason I find this attempt to turn the damage caused by bad weather, the hardship of families and the difficulties of communities into tools of electoral propaganda disgusting.”

    So says Fratelli d’Italia Senator from Abruzzo Etelwardo Sigismondi, group leader in the 8th Public Works Committee.

    Chieti, 10 November 2026.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.
