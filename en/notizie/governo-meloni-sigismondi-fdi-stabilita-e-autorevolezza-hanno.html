    Four years of the Meloni government: over three hundred people from Abruzzo at the Bari rally

    4 September 20262 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    On 4 September 2026, the day the Meloni government becomes the longest-serving in republican history, Sigismondi claims its results and announces Abruzzo’s participation in the Bari rally: over three hundred people and six coaches.

    Why it matters: The regional coordinator gauges local support: many participants hold no party card.

    “Today, 4 September, we celebrate a result of extraordinary importance, one that rewards the great work done by Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni. A significant milestone for Fratelli d’Italia and for the whole centre-right, but above all for Italy and for Italians.”

    So says Senator Etelwardo Sigismondi, regional coordinator of Fratelli d’Italia in Abruzzo.

    “The stability of the Government has allowed Italy to achieve fundamental results: over a million more people in work since the Executive took office, falling unemployment and rising female employment, a figure I am particularly keen to highlight. Taxes have been cut, putting more money in pay packets; unprecedented investment has gone into healthcare and important measures have been introduced to support families. Irregular migrant landings have fallen and repatriations have risen.

    Numerous contracts in the main sectors of public administration, such as healthcare, schools and defence, have also been unblocked, and more resources have been allocated to security, with more officers and more checks.

    At last people are once again looking at our nation with confidence and investing in Italy. Stability is a fundamental value because it allows a Government to deliver the electoral programme presented to the public. Today we celebrate not only the longest-serving Government in the history of the Italian Republic, but also the most stable Government in Europe. If in the past Italy was regarded as a weak nation, today it is seen as a strong one thanks to two fundamental elements: the stability of the majority and the authority of Prime Minister Giorgia Meloni.

    There is also a political reflection to be made: the statistics show that the three longest-serving Governments in republican history are the Meloni Government and two Executives led by Silvio Berlusconi. All of them centre-right Governments. It is a fact that deserves attention because it demonstrates the cohesion and unity of a coalition that is politically and programmatically solid. This is the great difference from the centre-left, which over the years has often brought together fragmented forces with the sole aim of winning elections, without managing to guarantee real governability.

    Abruzzo will be present in Bari today with over three hundred people, six coaches and many other participants travelling to the rally by car or train. What pleased me most is the convinced and spontaneous support of so many citizens who want to show their appreciation for the work of Giorgia Meloni, of Fratelli d’Italia and of the entire centre-right. Many people without a party card and not directly involved in politics asked to take part and will be with us. This fills us with great joy,” Sigismondi concludes.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.

    Original title of the press release: Meloni government, Sigismondi (FdI): “Stability and authority have restored strength and confidence to Italy. Over 300 people from Abruzzo in Bari”
