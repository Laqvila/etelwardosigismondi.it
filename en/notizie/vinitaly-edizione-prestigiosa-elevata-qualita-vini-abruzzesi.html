    Vinitaly 2023, Abruzzo wines on show for the international market

    3 April 20231 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    At Vinitaly 2023, the international wine and spirits fair, Abruzzo wineries show excellences exported all over the world, between tradition and innovation.

    A prestigious edition for Vinitaly2023, the international wine and spirits exhibition, which confirms itself as an extraordinary showcase of made in Italy around the world. In these days Abruzzo too is displaying its excellences, represented by wine-producing companies that over the years have managed to win over the international market, exporting the flavors of our land all over the world. Tradition and innovation, authenticity and experimentation, for a work that expresses the value of our region's identity and history. Abruzzo wines are increasingly synonymous with quality.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.
