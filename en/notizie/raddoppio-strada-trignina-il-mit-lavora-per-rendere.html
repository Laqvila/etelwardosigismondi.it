    Safer Trignina: the MIT considers the works in the Anas multi-year plan

    4 May 20232 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    Answering a parliamentary question on 4 May 2023, the MIT (Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport) is considering including the Fondo Valle Trigno road in the Mit-Anas multi-year plan.

    Why it matters: More safety on a heavy-freight road between Abruzzo and Molise, scene of many accidents.

    Part of the dossier: Infrastructure and transport links

    There is great attention from the Government on the issue of the safety of the Fondo Valle Trigno. The Undersecretary at the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport, Tullio Ferrante, this morning in fact assured – in reply to the parliamentary question signed by me and by Senator Costanzo Della Porta – the utmost attention in assessing the inclusion of the work in the next Mit-Anas multi-year plan”.

    “In acknowledging the strategic importance of the work, both for the volume of traffic and for the territories served, a clear commitment emerged to continue a constructive dialogue with the administrators of the territories concerned in Abruzzo and Molise – the senator reports – to raise safety levels, including the improvement of the lighting systems and of the junctions along the Fondo Valle. The infrastructure could be upgraded with a possible doubling, including through functional sections”.

    My remarks focused on the high danger level of this stretch of road, where I highlighted the critical issues of “a road built in the 1980s and conceived as an artery for local traffic but which, for years, has instead seen a considerable increase in the transit of vehicles, including heavy goods vehicles, for the transport of freight. A flow of traffic set to grow, considering the recent classification as ports of national significance granted to the harbours of Vasto and Termoli, an important recognition obtained thanks also to the efforts of the two Regional Presidents of Abruzzo and Molise, Marco Marsilio and Donato Toma.

    The Trignina must therefore be regarded as a fundamental artery for freight traffic, representing a most valuable connection between the Adriatic and Tyrrhenian corridors, and investing in it means investing in the safety of citizens and in the economic development of the territories, and it means countering the depopulation of inland areas. We are keeping faith with the commitment made to local administrators, bringing to the attention of the Senate and the Government all the problems of a road which, unfortunately, has caused numerous accidents and victims over the years.

    A road whose doubling project, although it has been discussed for almost twenty years, has never even seen the development of a technical and economic feasibility study. Today's reply to the parliamentary question demonstrates the attention that the Meloni government, in these first months of the legislature, is devoting to the issue”.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.
