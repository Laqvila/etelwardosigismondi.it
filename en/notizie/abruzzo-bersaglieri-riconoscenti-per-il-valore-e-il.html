    Bersaglieri: interregional rally marks 60 years of the Lanciano section

    23 April 20231 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    Abruzzo hosts the 5th interregional Bersaglieri rally for the 60th anniversary of the Lanciano section; the senator thanks the Corps for its work in Italy and abroad.

    “We are grateful to the Bersaglieri Corps for its high valour and its long and honoured history. We are therefore delighted that it was Abruzzo that hosted the 5th interregional gathering on the occasion of the 60th anniversary of the founding of the Lanciano Bersaglieri section. We thank this specialist branch of the infantry arm of the Italian Army for the constant work of the units deployed on national territory and of those assigned to military missions abroad, for the enormous prestige they bring to our Nation”.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.

    Original title of the press release: Abruzzo – Bersaglieri: Grateful for the valour and the work that bring prestige to our nation.
