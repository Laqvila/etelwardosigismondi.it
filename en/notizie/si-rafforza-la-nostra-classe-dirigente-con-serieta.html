    Our leadership class grows stronger. With seriousness, consistency, concreteness and credibility

    7 July 20232 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    I welcome with enthusiasm and great satisfaction the new members joining Fratelli d'Italia in the province of Chieti, in the persons of Tiziana Magnacca, former mayor of…

    I welcome with enthusiasm and great satisfaction the new members joining Fratelli d'Italia in the province of Chieti, in the persons of Tiziana Magnacca, former mayor of San Salvo and current president of the Municipal Council, Mimmo Budano, mayor of Villalfonsina, Felice Magnacca, former mayor of Castiglione Messer Marino and Claudio Mastronardi, municipal councillor of San Salvo.

    A day that fills me with joy, both politically and personally, since the path that binds me to many of them does not begin today but goes back to when we were kids, united by a strong passion for politics and for our territory. Today that path continues within the same party, with the aim of strengthening the great vessel of ideas and projects that we are, also in view of the upcoming regional elections, in which Fratelli d'Italia intends to confirm itself as the leader of the centre-right and the leading party in Abruzzo and, above all, to reconfirm the good governance of President Marco Marsilio at the helm of the Region.

    I consider Marsilio the best president Abruzzo has ever had, and I say it loud and clear also to the centre-left, which strains to attack the regional administration on all those issues on which the centre-left itself left disasters behind and which, over these four years, we have been working to repair, while at the same time providing concrete answers to the serious emergencies we found ourselves facing.

    Above all, this regional government has been able to provide this region with important paths and projects for revival, finally returning to centre stage in the national agenda. Our leadership class continues to grow stronger, with great administrative expertise, so that our party may increasingly be a point of reference for the territories, on the basis of principles that for us are sacrosanct: seriousness, consistency, concreteness and credibility, values that, thanks to President Giorgia Meloni, have brought us to be the leading party in Italy and that are now marking the incisive action of the national government.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.
