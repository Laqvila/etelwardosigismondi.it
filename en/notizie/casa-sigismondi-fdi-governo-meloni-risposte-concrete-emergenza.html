    Housing, 5% flat tax on agreed rents and the first-home Fund in the budget: “concrete answers to the housing emergency”

    18 November 20261 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    On 18 November 2026, at the press conference of the Fratelli d’Italia parliamentary groups on housing policies, Sigismondi presents the housing measures in the budget law under examination in Parliament. The flat tax on rents at agreed rates falls from 10 to 5 per cent, and to 1 per cent for tenants under 36, and the Guarantee Fund for first homes is refinanced to help young people obtain a mortgage.

    Why it matters: In Rome a mortgage instalment takes 36 per cent of income, in Milan up to 45, and rent exceeds on average 30 per cent of a salary. The measures are before Parliament and aim to reduce this burden for families and young people.

    “Housing is central to the action of the Meloni government, which over these four years, in clear break with the past, has chosen to tackle the housing emergency with structural measures, an emergency that is particularly acute in the big cities.

    Today the share of income needed to pay a mortgage instalment reaches 36% in Rome and peaks of 45% in Milan, and rent exceeds on average 30% of a salary for a 75-square-metre home. This economic pressure also affects the quality of housing, often forcing families to buy or rent homes smaller than they really need.

    We respond to these difficulties with concrete measures rather than announcements, including in the budget law: from the cut in the flat tax on rents at agreed rates, which falls from 10% to 5% and to 1% for contracts with tenants under 36, to the refinancing of the Guarantee Fund for first homes, a key tool to help young people obtain a mortgage. For Fratelli d’Italia a home is neither a luxury nor a privilege but the pillar on which the security and stability of Italian families are built.”

    So says Fratelli d’Italia Senator Etelwardo Sigismondi, group leader in the 8th Committee (Environment, ecological transition, energy, public works, communications, technological innovation) of the Senate, speaking at the press conference on housing policies held today in Rome by the Fratelli d’Italia parliamentary groups of the Chamber and the Senate.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.

    Original title of the press release: Housing, Sigismondi (FdI): “The Meloni government gives concrete answers to the housing emergency, support for families and young people”
