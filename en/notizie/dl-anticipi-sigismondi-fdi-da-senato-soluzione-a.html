    Strada dei Parchi: dispute settled through the advances decree

    1 December 20231 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    The Senate Budget Committee approves the amendment to the advances decree-law: Strada dei Parchi returns as A24-A25 concessionaire and tolls stay at 2017 levels.

    Why it matters: Over 1,300 Toto group employees protected and motorway tolls frozen at 2017 levels.

    Part of the dossier: Infrastructure and transport links

    “A historic mess was resolved today, with the approval in the Senate Budget Committee of the amendment that allows the Strada dei Parchi company of the
    Toto group to once again become the concessionaire of the A24-A25 motorway, which
    connects Abruzzo with Lazio. A dispute between the State and the company which thus comes to a close with the best solution, above all with a view to safeguarding the more than 1300 employees of the Toto group, who are granted 500 million, divided over the two years 2023 and 2024.

    An intervention that thus also makes it possible to freeze motorway toll rates at 2017 levels. Another considerable result achieved, which demonstrates, once again, the majority’s determination and effectiveness in tackling and settling even the thorniest issues, such as the management of this important motorway network has been.” This is what the Abruzzo senator of FdI, Etelwardo Sigismondi, declares

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.

    Original title of the press release: DL Anticipi, Sigismondi (FdI): From the Senate a solution to a historic mess over the Strada dei Parchi dispute
