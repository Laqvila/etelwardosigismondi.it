    River contracts, the Senate Environment Committee approves the Rosa, Sigismondi and Nocco resolution

    5 November 20261 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    On 4 November 2026 the Senate’s 8th Committee approves the resolution by Fratelli d’Italia senators Rosa, Sigismondi and Nocco on river contracts, the agreements that bring institutions, businesses, associations and citizens together to care for a river basin. The text commits the Government to regular maintenance of riverbeds and mouths, more weight for these measures in funding against hydrogeological instability and a stronger National Observatory.

    Why it matters: Italy has 83 signed river contracts and more than 200 in preparation. The resolution asks for them to become a permanent tool for preventing flood risk, with the territories in the front line.

    Yesterday, in the Senate’s 8th Committee, we approved a resolution devoted to strengthening the role of river contracts in sustainable development and in the prevention of flood risk.

    Water security is one of the great challenges of our time, and climate change makes the proper management of river basins ever more urgent. River contracts are a concrete tool for doing so. They bring institutions, businesses, associations and citizens around the same table and produce shared action programmes for the environmental regeneration of a watercourse’s basin, with real benefits for the territories. Today Italy has 83 signed contracts, with more than 200 processes under way.

    With this act we commit the Government to supporting and consolidating this experience, including by promoting sustainable and regular hydraulic maintenance of basins, riverbeds and river mouths, in order to reduce flood risk in the stretches most exposed for towns and infrastructure. We also ask for a better assessment, when state funds are allocated, of the measures against hydrogeological instability identified within river contracts. Finally, we ask for the National Observatory at the Ministry of the Environment to be strengthened, with more monitoring, coordination and training for public administrations.

    Particular attention goes to the educational and civic dimension. We want to use the river contract to bring the culture of water into schools and to promote women’s empowerment in the management of water resources.

    River contracts are an important tool for preventing flood risk, because they make it possible to stop chasing emergencies and because they directly involve citizens and territories in sustainable development and in protecting the environment for future generations, as Article 9 of the Constitution reminds us.

    We thank our colleagues on the Committee for their contribution and support.

    Rome, 5 November 2026.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.
