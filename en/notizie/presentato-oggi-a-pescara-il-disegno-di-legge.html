    Trabocchi, a bill to protect them and rebuild those that collapsed

    17 August 20231 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    Presented in Pescara on 17 August 2023, the bill on trabocchi: protection of the 32 existing ones, rebuilding of collapsed ones and exclusion from the EU directive on concessions.

    Why it matters: Collapsed or demolished trabocchi could be rebuilt on the same site.

    The trabocchi represent a distinctive feature of our territory: born as fishing machines, today they serve various functions, including that of restaurants. The trabocchi have managed to adapt to the times and evolve over the years; to date there are 32 in existence, 11 of which are protected by the Superintendency. We provide for the safeguarding of the existing ones and the possibility, for those that have collapsed or been demolished, of being rebuilt on the same site; it is further provided that all the trabocchi be excluded, for their preservation, from the European directive on concessions, which allows for the possibility of derogations precisely for the safeguarding of cultural heritage.

    Special thanks go to Prof. De Carolis for his fundamental contribution to the drafting of the law and to the sensitivity of the Marsilio administration, represented today by the Regional Councillor for State Property of the Abruzzo Region, Nicola Campiteli.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.

    Original title of the press release: The bill on the protection and safeguarding of the trabocchi presented today in Pescara.
