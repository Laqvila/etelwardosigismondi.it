    Abruzzo courts: enabling law on the judicial map on its way

    30 November 20231 min read

    Unofficial translation of the press release published in Italian.

    On 30 November 2023 Abruzzo mayors, provinces and bar councils meet the rapporteur of the courts decree: extension, staffing tables and an enabling law on the judicial map.

    Why it matters: Extension and new staffing tables aim to reopen and strengthen Abruzzo’s courts.

    Part of the dossier: The sub-provincial courts of Abruzzo

    “The Meloni government has made a commitment to a revision of the judicial geography, which it will carry forward in the coming months with a dedicated enabling law. These were the words of Senator Ernesto Rapani, rapporteur in committee for the decree on the new organization of the ordinary courts, with whom this afternoon we held the announced meeting with the Abruzzo mayors, the presidents of the Provinces of Chieti and L’Aquila and the presidents of the Bar Councils of the four seats of Justice.”

    This was announced by the Abruzzo members of Parliament of Fratelli d’Italia, Etelwardo Sigismondi, Guido Liris and Guerino Testa. “Central to the fruitful discussion – they report – was the issue of the extension understood as functional to the review of the judicial geography, in addition to the other significant aspect concerning the reopening of the staffing plans. Rapani, on this point, made it quite clear that the need to supplement the personnel is absolutely intrinsic to the ‘reopening’ of the courts and to their consequent strengthening.

    The announced presentation, by the government, of an ad hoc enabling law – they point out – will allow a faster review of the judicial geography. During the working session – the three Abruzzo parliamentarians comment – note was taken of the great impetus that, in this first year of the legislature, unlike in the past, the majority is giving to this complex matter, in order to reach a definitive solution to the problem. Keeping the representatives of our territory informed is a priority for us and we are satisfied with this important meeting, which demonstrates the great synergy between Abruzzo and Parliament.

    A personal thanks goes to our colleague Rapani, who made himself available from the outset, and for the thoroughness of his arguments,” they conclude.

    Source: press release published by the Senator and his staff.

    Original title of the press release: Abruzzo – courts. Sigismondi, Liris and Testa (FdI): Determination and concreteness from the Government. Fruitful meeting with mayors and lawyers.
