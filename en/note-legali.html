    Legal notice

    Nature of the site, responsibility for the content and transparency on the use of artificial intelligence.

    Nature of the website

    This site is the personal communication tool of Senator Etelwardo Sigismondi, published at etelwardosigismondi.it. It is not the official website of the Senate of the Republic or of any other institution: the official sources are listed on the Documents and acts page.

    Editorial responsibility

    The content is published under the responsibility of Senator Etelwardo Sigismondi, who edits it with his staff. The site is not a news publication under Italian Law no. 62 of 7 March 2001: it has no periodicity and is not subject to registration.

    Content sources and press releases

    Biographical and institutional content comes from verifiable public sources: the Senate of the Republic, the Chamber of Deputies, Openpolis, the Ministry of the Interior. Press releases are republished from the archive already made public by the Senator and his staff; the editorial headline sits alongside the original one without altering the facts. The press review gives headline, outlet, date and a short original summary, with a link to the article on the outlet's website: no article is reproduced. Versions in languages other than Italian are unofficial translations; in case of discrepancy the Italian text prevails.

    Images and rights in the content

    The photographs and the "L'Abruzzo in Parlamento" artwork come from material of the Senator's office already released on his official channels. The site's original texts are by Etelwardo Sigismondi: reproduction is permitted with attribution. Headlines, trademarks and articles cited in the press review belong to their respective outlets. Anyone who believes their rights in a piece of content have been infringed can report it to the address at the bottom of the page: the content will be checked and, where appropriate, removed.

    External links

    The site links to third-party websites (Senate, Chamber of Deputies, Openpolis, news outlets, social networks), marked as external. The Senator has no control over those sites and is not responsible for their content, their privacy policies or their availability.

    Transparency on the use of artificial intelligence

    In accordance with Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act), and in particular the transparency obligations of Article 50, please note that this site was designed and developed, and its texts written, with the help of artificial-intelligence tools, under the direction, review and editorial responsibility of people: every piece of content is checked by a human before publication. The English, French, Spanish and German versions are translations produced with the help of AI and reviewed. The site runs no AI system: no chatbot, no profiling, no automated decision-making. Messages sent through the report form are read by people and are not processed by AI systems.

    Limitation of liability

    The content serves information and political-communication purposes and is drafted with the utmost care; it is neither an official act nor advice. For the current state of acts, appointments and votes, the official sources of the Senate of the Republic and of the other bodies cited are authoritative. The Senator is not liable for damage arising from the use of the information published or from the temporary unavailability of the site.

    Governing law

    The site and these notes are governed by Italian law. For personal-data protection see the privacy notice and the cookie page.

    Reports and errors

    To report inaccuracies or request corrections or changes:
